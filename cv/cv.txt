DESIGN PROJECTS

Parametric Actuator and Gearbox Generators with Drawing Pack
(Mechanical design, GD&T, CadQuery)
  Wrote two parametric generators that size a design before drawing it: a
  hydraulic flight-control actuator family across four aircraft classes, and an
  engine accessory gearbox across five power ratings (5-50 kW), with involute
  gear geometry, Lewis bending-stress module sizing and SKF 60xx bearing
  selection. Produced a four-sheet A4 drawing pack - three detail drawings and a
  ballooned assembly GA - carrying ISO 286 fits (35 H8 bore, 21 f7 rod), ISO 1101
  geometric tolerances, a chosen datum scheme, surface finishes, a parts list and
  a five-contributor tolerance stack (390 mm nominal, worst case +/-0.90, RSS
  +/-0.44). Verification found six real defects, including a housing that did not
  enclose its own gear, and meshing teeth that physically intersected by 424 mm3
  in every exported assembly because the two gears were never phased - fixed by
  offsetting one by half a tooth pitch, which is parity-dependent and wrong to
  apply to an odd gear. Checked the test suite by reintroducing a known sign
  error: it is caught only by testing that the tooth narrows toward its tip,
  while pitch-circle thickness and solid-validity checks both still pass.
  132 automated tests.
  Tech: Python, CadQuery, OpenCASCADE, ISO 286, ISO 1101, STEP

Formed Sheet-Metal Equipment Bracket
(Sheet-metal design, DFM)
  Parametric formed bracket with its flat pattern derived from a K-factor neutral
  axis, not assumed - 101.52 mm blank against 105.00 mm of summed outside legs.
  Validated by conservation of volume: the formed solid and the flat blank agree
  to 0.25%, against 5.12% for a naively summed blank - a bound tightened from 2%
  after the looser one was found to be hiding two missing holes. Five
  design-for-manufacture
  rules (minimum bend radius, flange length, hole-to-bend distance, fastener edge
  distance, minimum hole diameter) with a material trade that has a real answer:
  2024-T3 is twice as strong as 5052-H32 and cannot make the fold, needing 4T
  minimum bend radius against 1T. Detail drawing with bend table; STEP and DXF
  export.
  Tech: Python, CadQuery, sheet-metal forming, DFM

Structural Sizing and Redesign of That Bracket
(FEA, mesh convergence, design trade study)
  Sized the bracket against a 9g equipment crash load in CalculiX, driven
  headlessly through FreeCAD. Ran a five-density mesh convergence study rather
  than a single solve, which is what made the result usable: deflection converged
  to 0.06% and bulk stress to 0.28%, while peak stress climbed 12% and never
  settled - a singularity at the edge of a fixed constraint, so the 545 MPa
  headline is a boundary condition rather than a stress. The converged 196.7 MPa
  against a 193 MPa yield condemns the part marginally, not by the 2.8x a single
  mesh reports. Screened four fixes analytically before modelling any: stiffening
  flanges up the upright make that leg 7.4x stronger and change the margin by
  nothing, because both legs carry the same moment through the same section and
  the governing stress moves to the untouched base. Selected 2.0 mm gauge, solved
  it to confirm (126.1 MPa, +53% margin, deflection halved), and re-issued the
  drawing - the bend deduction changes with gauge, so a shop cutting the old
  blank would make the wrong part. Cross-checked against closed-form beam theory
  sharing no code, which reads 16% conservative at both gauges to within 0.2 of a
  point. 66 automated tests.
  Tech: CalculiX, gmsh, FreeCAD FEM, Python, CadQuery
