Parametric Actuator and Gearbox Generators with Drawing Pack
(Mechanical design, GD&T, CadQuery)
  Wrote two parametric generators that size a design before drawing it: a
  hydraulic flight-control actuator family across four aircraft classes, and an
  engine accessory gearbox across five power ratings (5-50 kW), with involute
  gear geometry, Lewis bending-stress module sizing and SKF 60xx bearing
  selection. Produced a four-sheet A4 drawing pack - three detail drawings and a
  ballooned assembly GA - carrying ISO 286 fits (35 H8 bore, 21 f7 rod), ISO 1101
  geometric tolerances, a chosen datum scheme, surface finishes, a parts list and
  a five-contributor tolerance stack (390 mm nominal, worst case +/-0.90, RSS
  +/-0.44). Verification found five real defects, including a mirrored involute
  profile that produced an invalid solid and a housing that did not enclose its
  own gear. 83 automated tests.
  Tech: Python, CadQuery, OpenCASCADE, ISO 286, ISO 1101, STEP

Formed Sheet-Metal Equipment Bracket
(Sheet-metal design, DFM)
  Parametric formed bracket with its flat pattern derived from a K-factor neutral
  axis, not assumed - 101.52 mm blank against 105.00 mm of summed outside legs.
  Validated by conservation of volume: the formed solid and the flat blank agree
  to 1.07%, against 5.12% for a naively summed blank. Five design-for-manufacture
  rules (minimum bend radius, flange length, hole-to-bend distance, fastener edge
  distance, minimum hole diameter) with a material trade that has a real answer:
  2024-T3 is twice as strong as 5052-H32 and cannot make the fold, needing 4T
  minimum bend radius against 1T. Detail drawing with bend table; STEP and DXF
  export.
  Tech: Python, CadQuery, sheet-metal forming, DFM

Installation Aerodynamics of Aero-Engine Nacelles
Cranfield Individual Research Project (Parametric CAD, CFD)

Twin-Spool Turbofan Cycle Model
