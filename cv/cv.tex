\section{Design Projects}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Parametric Actuator and Gearbox Generators with Drawing
  Pack} --- \textbf{(Mechanical design, GD\&T, CadQuery)}:
  Wrote two parametric generators that size a design before drawing it: a
  hydraulic flight-control actuator family across four aircraft classes,
  and an engine accessory gearbox across five power ratings (5--50~kW),
  with involute gear geometry, Lewis bending-stress module sizing and
  SKF 60xx bearing selection. Produced a four-sheet A4 drawing pack ---
  three detail drawings and a ballooned assembly GA --- carrying ISO~286
  fits (\O{}35~H8 bore, \O{}21~f7 rod), ISO~1101
  geometric tolerances, a chosen datum scheme, surface finishes, a parts
  list and a five-contributor tolerance stack (390~mm nominal, worst case
  $\pm$0.90, RSS $\pm$0.44). Verification found five real defects,
  including a mirrored involute profile that produced an invalid solid
  and a housing that did not enclose its own gear. 83 automated tests.
  \textit{Tech: Python, CadQuery, OpenCASCADE, ISO 286, ISO 1101, STEP}

\item \textbf{Formed Sheet-Metal Equipment Bracket} ---
  \textbf{(Sheet-metal design, DFM)}:
  Parametric formed bracket with its flat pattern derived from a
  K-factor neutral axis, not assumed --- 101.52~mm blank against 105.00~mm
  of summed outside legs. Validated by conservation of volume: the formed
  solid and the flat blank agree to 1.07\%, against 5.12\% for a naively
  summed blank. Five design-for-manufacture rules (minimum bend radius,
  flange length, hole-to-bend distance, fastener edge distance, minimum
  hole diameter) with a material trade that has a real answer: 2024-T3 is
  twice as strong as 5052-H32 and cannot make the fold, needing 4T
  minimum bend radius against 1T. Detail drawing with bend table; STEP
  and DXF export. \textit{Tech: Python, CadQuery, sheet-metal forming, DFM}
\end{itemize}

\section{Propulsion and Analysis Projects}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Installation Aerodynamics of Aero-Engine Nacelles} ---
  \textbf{Cranfield Individual Research Project (Parametric CAD, CFD)}:
  Fitted a Class-Shape Transformation curve directly to NASA TM 110300's
  published NACA 1-85-100 cowl ordinates, reproducing them to 0.019\% RMS
  of maximum radius. Built and converged a compressible RANS OpenFOAM
  case (rhoSimpleFoam, $k$-$\omega$ SST) at Mach 0.79, meshed with
  snappyHexMesh at 108,904 cells, and compared surface pressure against
  the same report's wind-tunnel data --- RMS $C_p$ error 0.65 over 17
  matched stations, reported including where the model misses the
  leading-edge suction peak.
  \textit{Tech: pyOCC, Open CASCADE, iCST, OpenFOAM, Python (Ongoing,
  expected Sep 2026)}

\item \textbf{Twin-Spool Turbofan Cycle Model} ---
  \textbf{(Gas turbine performance)}:
  Station-by-station separate-exhaust cycle solver with every turbine
  pressure ratio matched to the power its own spool demands rather than
  assumed. 57.67~kN net thrust, 46.7\% thermal efficiency at the
  reference design point, validated against the closed-form ideal-Brayton
  limit to within 0.01\%. 82 tests.
  \textit{Tech: Python, Matplotlib, pytest, MATLAB/Simulink cross-check}

\item \textbf{Investigation of Controlled Jets for Enhanced Mixing Rates}
  --- \textbf{BEng Final Year Project, team of 3 (CFD, experimental
  validation)}: Investigated passive flow-control tab geometries (Delta
  Tandem Tab, M Delta Tandem Tab) for jet mixing using ANSYS Fluent
  across Mach 0.6--1.0, with grid-independence studies to 3.4M+ nodes,
  validated against experimental shadowgraph imaging. Achieved 64--76\%
  core-length reduction against an uncontrolled baseline at Mach 0.8 with
  no significant thrust penalty.
  \textit{Tech: ANSYS Fluent, ANSYS DesignModeler, ANSYS ICEM CFD (Mar 2025)}
\end{itemize}
