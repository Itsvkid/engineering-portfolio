\project{Parametric Actuator and Accessory-Gearbox Generators, with Drawing Pack}{mechanical design, GD\&T}{2026}
\begin{itemize}
\item Sized before drawn --- a flight-control actuator family across four aircraft classes and an accessory gearbox across five ratings (5--50\,kW), with involute gear geometry, Lewis bending-stress sizing and SKF 60xx bearings --- then issued as a four-sheet A4 pack: three details and a ballooned GA with ISO 286 fits (35\,H8 bore, 21\,f7 rod), ISO 1101 tolerances, a datum scheme, a parts list and a five-contributor stack on 390\,mm nominal (worst case $\pm$0.90, RSS $\pm$0.44).
\item Verification found six real defects, including meshing teeth intersecting by 424\,mm\textsuperscript{3} in every exported assembly because the gears were never phased --- then the test suite was checked against itself with a reintroduced sign error, which only the tooth-taper test catches. 132 tests.
\end{itemize}

\project{Formed Sheet-Metal Equipment Bracket, Sized and Redesigned}{sheet metal, DFM, FEA}{2026}
\begin{itemize}
\item Flat pattern from a K-factor neutral axis rather than assumed (101.52\,mm blank against 105.00\,mm of summed legs), validated by conservation of volume to 0.25\% against 5.12\% for a naive blank; five DFM rules, and a material trade with a real answer --- 2024-T3 is twice as strong as 5052-H32 and cannot make the fold, 4T bend radius against 1T.
\item Sized against a 9g crash load in CalculiX, where a five-density convergence study made it usable: peak stress climbed 12\% and never settled --- a singularity at a fixed constraint, so the 545\,MPa headline is a boundary condition, not a stress, and the converged 196.7\,MPa against 193\,MPa yield condemns the part marginally rather than by 2.8$\times$. Four fixes were screened by hand first --- stiffening the upright makes that leg 7.4$\times$ stiffer and changes the margin by nothing --- before 2.0\,mm gauge (126.1\,MPa, +53\%) and a re-issued drawing, the bend deduction having moved with it. 66 tests.
\end{itemize}

\project{Nacelle Cowl Geometry and CFD Validation}{personal project, public data}{2026}
CST curve fitted to NASA TM 110300's published NACA 1-85-100 cowl ordinates to 0.019\% RMS of max radius: a compressible RANS OpenFOAM case ($k$-$\omega$ SST, 108{,}904 cells) at Mach 0.79 matched to that report's wind-tunnel pressures to RMS $C_p$ 0.65 over 17 stations, including where it misses the suction peak. \textit{Separately: a Cranfield research project on nacelle installation aerodynamics, on an industry partner's geometry data --- confidential, result pending.}

\project{NASA/GE E\textsuperscript{3} Turbofan, Rebuilt from its Design Reports}{gas turbine performance, turbomachinery}{2026}
\begin{itemize}
\item Nine of ten planned work stages complete on a twin-spool turbofan reproduced from public-domain NASA reports --- cycle, blading, cooling, secondary air, stress. Every value carries its report page and every solver states the tolerance it must meet \textit{before} it runs: HPC efficiency lands 0.15 of a point from the published value on a 2.0-point band, while engine sfc lands at 1.91\% against a 1.5\% band --- a miss, recorded as one rather than rounded into a pass. Over 1{,}000 automated tests. Published as an interactive 3D anatomy at \href{https://vinaykumar.is-a.dev/turbofan}{\textbf{vinaykumar.is-a.dev/turbofan}} --- 144 procedural parts, every fact on it tagged to a report page or flagged schematic or assumed --- with a six-sheet drawing pack behind it.
\item \textbf{ME TF0.01} --- a derivative trade \textit{study} of my own on that fixed core, never built: what bypass ratio 10 costs. Fan and LP turbine want the LP shaft 46\% apart at BPR 10, against 0.7\% at the E\textsuperscript{3}'s own 6.7 --- the geared-fan argument as a number, and on this core the answer is a longer LP turbine, not a 1.46:1 gearbox: eight LP stages direct-drive against five geared, and the tip-Mach ceiling written down before the run rejected my own first answer of seven. Fuel burn is a 3--4.5\% band, not the 4.5\% headline, because fan efficiency is held at the E\textsuperscript{3}'s values at a different tip Mach --- worth 0.68\% of sfc a point.
\end{itemize}
